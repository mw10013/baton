# Billing slice: research

Status: research, written 2026-10-18. **Settled** 2026-10-18 (§0): Q1–Q17 as recommended in review round 1. No code has changed.

The work itself: the plan gate on `/app` and on every socket; the Partner API plan read with legacy's 24 h cache, its boundary clamp, its Manage plan window and its daily cron; counted orders and the usage-event outbox with its flush to the App Events API; the home page's **Your plan** section (two meter tiles and Manage plan); `QuotaBanners` on the home page and the orders index; the member half of access (a lapsed shop's members land on **Subscription inactive**). It is roadmap §3 row G. The admin console's plan and usage fields are H; the help pages are I.

Legacy paths are relative to `refs/baton-legacy/`. Legacy's billing words are its vocabulary table on `src/lib/domain/Billing.ts` (plan, app subscription, billing cycle, trial, meter, counted order, seat, included, usage event, expired event); they port unchanged.

### 1.1 What legacy does

**The plan.** Two public plans on App Pricing, `baton-basic` and `baton-pro`, each $29 / $79 a month with a 14-day trial and two graduated meters (`production-orders`, `members`). Entitlements: Basic 200 orders and 3 members included, Pro 1,500 and 10. The app reads a shop's app subscription with the Partner API's `activeSubscription` and caches the handle on the D1 `ShopSession` row:

- fresh for 24 h, clamped to the app subscription's boundary (cycle end, or trial end) plus 5 min skew; a past boundary is ignored;
- a handle outside the allowlist, a never-fetched row or a past deadline is a miss and re-reads; a null handle inside the deadline is a verified "Unsubscribed";
- no `ShopSession` row is `Unsubscribed` without a Partner call;
- a Partner, D1 or decode failure is `SubscriptionPlanError`, never `Unsubscribed` ("we could not ask" is never "you have not paid");
- each revalidation writes the three columns, revokes every socket of the shop when the handle changed (not on the first fetch), and pushes the billing cycle and the D1 member count to the object outside a trial.

**The gate.** `/app`'s `beforeLoad` resolves the plan (or refreshes it when the search carries `plan_handle`, Shopify's billing redirect back to the plan's welcome link). `Unsubscribed` redirects to `/auth/exit-iframe?exitIframe=<pricing URL>&shop=<shop>`, the pricing URL being `https://admin.shopify.com/store/<store>/charges/<app handle>/pricing_plans`. `Subscribed` answers the plan and `managePlanUrl` into the route context. The socket gate answers `402` for an unsubscribed merchant and for a member of a lapsed shop; the tab's socket, on the revoke's `3401`, invalidates the router so `beforeLoad` runs again and redirects. Legacy has **no plan bypass** for dev or e2e: development stores in the same Partner organization get every public plan at $0, and the dev and e2e stores hold a real subscription. The integration tests seed the plan cache instead.

**Counting** (the triggers table on `ShopUsage`, 12 rows, ported verbatim). An order is counted once, when its first run is created: `countedAt` set, `ordersThisCycle` +1, one `UsageEvent` (`<orderId>#count`, value 1, dated now). The cycle is resolved first: no cycle opens a provisional one at the UTC month's start; a count past `cycleEndAt` rolls the cycle forward open-ended and recounts from `countedAt`. Fixture orders are counted at 0, so they never reach the meter.

**The billing cycle push** (`setBillingCycle`). A changed start recounts from rows, resets the seat mark to the member count and queues it whole, dated at the cycle start (`seat#<start>#<count>`); on a shop never addressed, every event before the start is dropped. An unchanged start raises the seat mark to a higher member count and queues the rise. Then a flush.

**The flush.** Nothing without a `shopGid` and a cycle (the rows wait). Expired rows (dated before the cycle) deleted and logged, up to `sweepBatch`; then up to `sweepBatch` live rows oldest first, each posted on its own; a 2xx deletes the row, a refusal bumps `attempts` and records `lastError`. Never inside a transaction; never raises (logged); answers sent and remaining. The App Events client mints a client-credentials token with the app's key and secret, caches it per instance (refresh 5 min early, one hour assumed when `expires_in` is absent), retries once on a `401` with a fresh token, sends a `User-Agent` (Workers' `fetch` sends none and Shopify's edge answers `403` without one).

**Screens.**

- **The home page's Your plan** (`<s-section heading="Your plan">`, after the Orders strip): `Tiles` of two `MeterTile`s, then Manage plan. "Orders this billing cycle": "`N` of `M` included", a `<progress max=M value=N>`, a detail line "`K` over. Extra orders are billed at your plan's rate." and "Resets `<date>`." when the cycle end is known. "Members": "`N` members, `M` included" from the D1 member count, and "`K` over. Extra members are billed at your plan's rate." No tier is named anywhere.
- **Manage plan** (`ManagePlanButton`): secondary button; a click runs the POST server function (`expectChange`) and opens the pricing URL in `_top` when it answers, fails, or after 1.5 s.
- **`QuotaBanners`**: one critical banner when `ordersLimitedAt` is set, "New orders stopped syncing at 2,500 open orders. Fulfill or cancel orders in Shopify, then Sync open orders."; on the home page and the orders index. There is deliberately no banner for going past the included orders.
- **Subscription inactive** (`/shop/$shop/lapsed`, outside the member layout): heading the shop, section "Subscription inactive", "This store’s Baton subscription is not active, so its workflows are unavailable. Ask the merchant to renew the subscription from the Baton app in their Shopify admin.", a link to Your stores.

### 1.4 Risks

- **The gate can lock everyone out.** One wrong value (org id, app id, handle, token, API version) turns every `/app` load into an error page and every socket into `503` on that stage. The plan's first step is a live spike against the real Partner API for Baton Dev on `mw10013-e2e-shop-00`, before any gate code lands.
- **Every suite and the dev server start depending on a real subscription** (Q14). An unsubscribed dev or e2e store redirects to the pricing page, which sits behind a Cloudflare bot check that challenges headless browsers. A store's subscription is a human step, once per store.
- **Staging needs Shopify-side setup first** (Q15): plans exist only on an app whose distribution is public, and choosing a distribution is permanent.
- **Shopify's answers differ from legacy's notes** (Q6): App Events now documents a non-2xx answer with a reason and a 24-hour idempotency window, where legacy wrote "`202` to everything" and "permanent".
- **Counting inside the order transaction.** `countOrder` runs on `syncTransaction`'s sync scheduler with the input gate closed (F's reconcile risk): storage only, no network, no D1.
- **Orders counted before G.** F's runs created no counts; staging's order #1003 has runs and a null `countedAt`, and stays uncounted unless a new item gets a run (Q16).
- **Manage plan's flush outliving the click.** Legacy relied on `ctx.waitUntil` in its Worker. Here the browser navigates `_top` away after 1.5 s and the Website's server function awaits a Backend call; whether the Backend call survives the client going away needs a check (the plan's Manage plan step starts with it).

## 5. Questions and recommendations

1. **Where does it run?** Nothing else is in flight; `wt-01` and `wt-02` are clean. The meter (sub-slice 2) and the plan (3) touch disjoint files at first but meet in the actor's wiring and `ShopActors`. **Recommendation:** one implementation thread on `main`, sub-slices in order, a `CHECKPOINT` after sub-slice 0 (the spike's answers decide configuration) and after 3 (the Backend half).

2. **Names.** Legacy split the meter across `OrderRepository` (storage) and `BillingAgent` (flush, usage, cycle push). Roadmap §0.2 names by role. **Recommendation:** `makeUsageRepository(sql, transaction)` in `shop-actor/usage-repository.ts` with legacy's method names (`countOrder`, `setBillingCycle`, `flushUsageEvents`, `getUsage`, the privates `currentCycle`, `countedSince`, `raiseSeatMark`, `queueUsageEvent`) and `makeBilling({ usageRepository, appEvents, shop })` in `shop-actor/billing.ts` holding `BillingAgent`'s three (`flushUsageEvents` logged and never raised, `getUsage` with `databaseSize`, `setBillingCycle` then flush); log prefix `Billing.`. The order repository keeps the ceiling's `markOrdersLimited`, `clearOrdersLimited`, `countOpenOrders`, `lastSweepAt`, which are orders' load rules, not billing. Backend: `ShopifyPartner` (`src/backend/shopify-partner.ts`), `SubscriptionPlan` (`subscription-plan.ts`), `ShopifyAppEvents` (`shopify-app-events.ts`), legacy's names.

3. **The domain.** **Recommendation:** port legacy `domain/Billing.ts` whole into `src/domain/billing.ts`, its vocabulary table on the module's top symbol, `AdminShopPlanCache` included (H reads it); `ShopUsage` keeps legacy's triggers table with "pinned by" titles. `planHandleExpiresAt` and its constants stay in `SubscriptionPlan` as legacy has them.

4. **How the `/app` gate's answer reaches the browser.** Legacy's `beforeLoad` ran Effect in the Worker and threw TanStack's `redirect`. Here `authenticateApp` is a server function over `BackendApi.authenticateAdmin`, which answers `{ apiKey, shop, … }` today. **Recommendation:** `authenticateAdmin` takes `billingRedirect` (legacy's `location.searchStr.includes("plan_handle")`), resolves or refreshes after authenticating, and answers a tagged union: `Subscribed` with `managePlanUrl` (and no plan, legacy's "the browser never names its own tier"), or `Unsubscribed` with the exit-iframe href; a plan failure stays an error (the error page, never the redirect). `beforeLoad` throws `redirect({ href })` on `Unsubscribed`. The home page's numbers come from their own loader (`homePlan`), which resolves again as legacy's `resolveEntitlements` did, and redirects the same way when the entry expired between the two.

5. **The socket on a lapse.** Legacy's gate answered the upgrade `402`; the client treated a revoke's `3401` as "ask again" and invalidated the router. Here a refusal at the gate becomes a close code (`ShopAccessDenied` → the upgrade route), and the actor's `revoke` closes by tag. **Recommendation:** `ShopAccessDenied` grows `402`; the upgrade route closes it with a new terminal code, `CONNECTION_CLOSE_PAYMENT_REQUIRED` (4402), on which `ShopSocket` stops reconnecting and invalidates the router (`/app`'s `beforeLoad` redirects to plan selection; the member layout's loader redirects to the lapsed page). `revoke` grows `by: "all"`, closing every socket with `3401`, so a reconnect meets the gate's new answer. Membership is checked before the plan, as legacy, so a stranger still gets `404`.

6. **API versions, and Shopify's changed answers.** Legacy pinned the Partner API at `2026-07` because `activeSubscription` existed nowhere else; the docs now list it on the stable `2026-10` (shopify.dev, Partner API `2026-10`, `activeSubscription`). App Events is `2026-10` too, and its docs now say a rejected request is non-2xx with `{ success: false, error, errors[] }`, a billing event outside the cycle fails, and the idempotency window is 24 hours, where legacy's JSDoc says "`202` to everything" and "permanent". **Recommendation:** both at `2026-10`, set by the spike (sub-slice 0) against the live endpoints; the behaviour stays legacy's (a non-2xx keeps the row with `lastError`, now carrying Shopify's reason; expired rows deleted), and the JSDoc that states Shopify's answers is corrected to what the spike measured, dated. The 24-hour window changes nothing in practice: a row is deleted right after Shopify accepts it, so a replay is the same flush.

7. **Configuration.** Legacy kept `SHOPIFY_PARTNER_ORG_ID`, `SHOPIFY_PARTNER_APP_ID`, `SHOPIFY_APP_HANDLE` and `SHOPIFY_APP_EVENTS_API_VERSION` as wrangler vars per environment and the token in `.env`. **Recommendation:** the token is a secret: `SHOPIFY_PARTNER_API_TOKEN` in each checkout's untracked `.env` for dev and e2e (read through Alchemy `Config` like `SHOPIFY_DEV_STORE`), and a new `StageConfigValues` field for staging (the stage config runbook's "Add a value", before the push). Org id, app id and app handle are facts about a Shopify app, not secrets: a constant per app beside `ShopifyApp`'s choice in `src/backend/stack.ts` (Baton Dev's from legacy's `wrangler.jsonc`; Baton Staging's from Q15), bound with the `Shopify` binding. The API versions are code constants. `test` binds fakes and no token.

8. **Entitlements on the screen.** **Recommendation:** as legacy: the Backend compares, the actor never sees the plan; `homePlan` answers `{ entitlements, usage, memberCount, managePlanUrl }`; the Members tile counts D1 members now, not `seatsThisCycle`.

9. **Uninstall.** Legacy flushed before destroying, so a usage event queued in the uninstall's last moments still reached Shopify. **Recommendation:** port it: the uninstall path calls `ShopActors.flushUsageEvents` (best-effort, the remainder logged as legacy's line) and then `destroy`.

10. **The usage read: first render and live.** Legacy's `getUsage` was a merchant callable read by the home page and the orders index (both show `QuotaBanners`), and by loaders. `ordersLimitedAt` changes on a refused order (an `ORDERS_KEY` write path) and clears when Sync open orders starts (a `SYNC_KEY` write); `ordersThisCycle` changes on a counted order (`ORDERS_KEY`). **Recommendation:** first render through `homePlan` (home) and a usage field on the orders index's loader answer; live through `MerchantGetUsage`, an atom keyed on `ORDERS_KEY` and `SYNC_KEY`, seeded from the loaders. No new notice key.

11. **Notices from the billing cycle push.** `setBillingCycle` may recount `ordersThisCycle` (a new cycle) while the home page is open. **Recommendation:** the push notifies `ORDERS_KEY` when it changed the count or the cycle, so the meter re-reads; nothing else in this slice notifies.

12. **`DevRpcs`.** Legacy's dev store held a real subscription; the admin page's Refresh plan (H) forced a re-read. **Recommendation:** none grow. Manage plan's 15-minute window and `plan_handle`'s refresh cover a dev plan switch; H brings Refresh plan.

13. **Fakes for integ.** Legacy's integration tests seeded the plan cache and patched `fetch` for App Events. Here integ runs a deployed test Backend. **Recommendation:** a Partner fake and an App Events fake in the test Backend, beside `ShopifyFake`, scripted through `IntegRpcs` (`ScriptPartner`, `AppEventsSent`); the Partner fake answers `Subscribed` on `baton-pro` with a cycle for every shop unless scripted, so no existing test changes. Unit tests stub the services directly.

14. **Dev and e2e stores need real subscriptions.** Legacy had no bypass: its dev store held a $0 subscription, and plan switching ran headed by hand (`pnpm test:e2e:billing`) because the pricing page's bot check challenges headless browsers. Here the dev server and the e2e suite run on Baton Dev (legacy's own app, with its plans) across `mw10013-dev-shop-NN` and `mw10013-e2e-shop-NN`. **Recommendation:** no bypass, as legacy. A human step, once per store, recorded in `docs/shopify-stores-runbook.md` ("Set up a checkout's stores"): open the app on the store, pick a plan on the pricing page, approve. Now for `dev-shop-00` and `e2e-shop-00`; a worktree's stores when it is set up. The e2e setup project checks the e2e store's plan once and fails with that instruction instead of 60 redirected specs. The billing spec ports as its own headed project (`vp run test:e2e:billing`), outside the default suite. This needs you to confirm that Baton Dev's Partner app (org `2798461`, app `417214922753`, handle `baton-local` in legacy) still has `baton-basic` and `baton-pro` with both meters, and that the `mw10013-*` stores are development stores of that same organization.

15. **Staging's Shopify app.** `activeSubscription` works only for a public app with an App Pricing contract; Baton Staging was made in the Dev Dashboard and its toml sets no handle. Legacy planned a `baton-staging` app with its own plans. Choosing an app's distribution is permanent. **Recommendation:** make Baton Staging a public (unlisted) app in the Partner Dashboard, create legacy's two plans and meters on it (legacy README's table, verbatim), record its app id and handle in code (Q7), put the Partner token in `.env.staging` and the stage config record, and subscribe `mw10013-staging-shop-00`. These are your steps in Shopify's dashboards; the plan lists them in order, ahead of the staging push. The alternative, staging without a gate, would leave the gate unverified anywhere but local workerd, which slice A's `storage.sync()` showed is not enough.

16. **Orders counted before G.** F's runs counted nothing. **Recommendation:** no backfill and no reset: the only shop with such runs is staging's test store (order #1003); dev and e2e data is the fixture (counted at 0) or created per spec. A shop whose order already has a run is simply not billed for it, which is the safe direction.

17. **A runbook.** Legacy's README §Billing is the operator's guide: the plans table, the dashboard path, the credentials, "enabling an environment". **Recommendation:** `docs/billing-runbook.md` (rare, confusing, critical: the runbooks rule), porting that section: creating plans, the token, subscribing a store, switching a plan by hand, reading the Dev Dashboard's App Events log. `ENTITLEMENTS`' JSDoc keeps legacy's rule that its numbers and the Partner Dashboard's tiers change together, without pointing at the runbook.
