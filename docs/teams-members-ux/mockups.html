Teams & Members, reworked on the Workflows pattern

Five screens. Create goes behind a header button and a dialog, rename and delete move into More actions, the team page lists only its own people, and the step table becomes one line of links. There is no member page and no name field, on purpose. Reasoning is in docs/teams-members-ux-research.md.

Seed data shown. Numbered pins map to the notes under each screen.

3 · Team page

The team page is about people. Its own members are a table under a Members heading, adding is a header action, and the workflows that use the team are an aside card instead of a step table above the roster. Laid out as Polaris’ details template, like the order and workflow detail pages: main column for the roster, aside for the facts a merchant checks before a rename or delete. The page is inlineSize="base" — s-page renders the aside slot at no other width.

7 · What is deliberately not built

No member detail page

A member is an email, a short list of teams, a date and one destructive action. That is a table row. A page would have a breadcrumb, a heading that is an email, and the same two modals the row menu already opens.

What would change this: per-member work history or activity. Not asked for, not in scope.

No name field on members

Only one line in the whole product shows one person’s identity to another (“In progress since … by jenny@…” on the queue). The merchant typed the email and knows whose it is; the member reads their own. A name would add a migration, a field, a rename modal and a fallback rule at every render site to improve that one line.

If it ever comes, it should be self-service in the member area and called Name, not something the merchant maintains.

No step list on the team page

Replaced by the “Used by” aside card. Steps are edited in the workflow editor; the delete dialog already counts them. A list keyed by step grows without bound and pushes the people, the thing this page is for, below the fold.
